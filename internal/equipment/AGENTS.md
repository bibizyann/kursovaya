# Equipment model guidance

- This package is a pure reference inventory and deterministic simulation model; do not add Kubernetes or transport clients.
- Recipe numbers are educational fixtures, not manufacturer recommendations or qualified production parameters.
- Keep equipment identity, workstation membership, recipe settings, and operation results distinct.
- Changes to catalog IDs or capability names must update sample templates, Kind labels, documentation, and consistency tests.
- Catalog additions must define a device-specific operation and both product recipes.
